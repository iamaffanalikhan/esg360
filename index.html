Why this exists

ESG reporting has moved down the supply chain.

Disclosure rules started with the largest listed companies: Europe's CSRD, ISSB-based standards, India's BRSR, and climate laws such as the UAE's. Those companies' buyers, lenders and auditors now ask the same questions of the suppliers, developers and manufacturers who sell to them. Most of those firms have no ESG team and no rating.

ESG360 gives them a starting point. Answer in plain Yes, In progress or Not yet. Get a score you can explain line by line, a list of the gaps that matter most in your sector, and a benchmark against your peers.

Questions

Common questions

Is this an ESG rating?

No. It is a structured self-assessment. The score reflects your own answers and is not third-party assured. It does not replace a sustainability report, an assurance engagement or a lender's due diligence. The Verified tier adds an evidence review.

Why does my sector get different questions?

Materiality differs by industry. Effluent treatment is critical for a pharma plant and irrelevant for a software firm. Every company answers a common core, plus questions specific to its sector, and each is weighted to what matters most in that industry.

What counts as "In progress"?

The practice is underway, partly implemented, or committed with a funded plan, but not yet fully operating. It earns partial credit.

Why is Supply Chain shown separately?

Supply chain risk is reported as its own lens so you can see it clearly and act on it directly.

Where do my answers go?

Scoring runs entirely in your browser. A draft of your answers is kept only in this browser so you can come back to it. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to email us your results.

Can I keep a copy of my results?

Yes. The results screen lets you download a PDF report, copy a short summary to share, and note your certificate ID.

Does it work outside India and the UAE?

Yes. The assessment is built on the UN SDGs and globally recognised responsible-business principles, and questions refer to local regulators in general terms. Pick your country on the first step.

How are sector benchmarks set?

Benchmarks are reference baselines for each sector, built from published disclosures. They are directional and are refreshed as each sector's sample grows.

Can I see the full scoring model?

The weights, formula and benchmark data are proprietary to ESG360. ESG360 Verified clients get a full breakdown of their own score.

Our approach

Built on the frameworks your stakeholders already use.

ESG360 brings the UN Sustainable Development Goals, internationally recognised responsible-business principles and sector materiality together in one proprietary scoring model. Here is what goes into it.

FRAMEWORKS

Anchored to global frameworks

Every question maps to a UN Sustainable Development Goal and to a responsible-business principle. The practices assessed are the ones frameworks such as GRI, ISSB, CSRD and BRSR ask about.

MATERIALITY

Twelve sector modules

What is critical for a pharma plant is not what is critical for a bank. Each sector has its own questions and its own priorities.

ANSWERS

Three plain answers

Yes, In progress or Not yet. Work that is underway earns partial credit, so companies in transition are not penalised.

LENSES

Five lenses

Environment, Social, Governance, Disclosure & Compliance and Supply Chain, brought together in one score out of 100.

BENCHMARKS

Compared with your sector

Your score and each lens are placed against reference baselines for your industry.

CONSISTENCY

Rule-based and repeatable

The same answers always give the same score. No AI guesswork in the scoring.
